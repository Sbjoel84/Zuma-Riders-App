Identity & access

13 tables · 16 foreign keys

Users, profiles, sessions, OTP, devices and role-based permissions.

Refresh tokens rotate; reuse of a revoked token revokes the whole session family. Closing an account scrubs PII but keeps the User row so trips and ledger history stay intact.

Rides & negotiation

10 tables · 25 foreign keys

From the passenger's proposed fare through driver offers to the confirmed trip.

A Ride row is created only when the passenger accepts an offer. Unique keys on rideRequestId and acceptedOfferId, plus a partial unique index on active rides per driver, make double assignment impossible in the database. agreedFare is locked by trigger.

Wallets & ledger

7 tables · 12 foreign keys

Double-entry journal; wallet balances are caches of ledger accounts.

Every money movement is a LedgerTransaction whose entries balance (deferred trigger). Wallet balances are caches updated in the same transaction. Commission is unique per ride, so it cannot be charged twice.
